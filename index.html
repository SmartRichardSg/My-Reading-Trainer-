Why this app exists
This is a personal reading-improvement experiment, not a race. The aim is to explore a comfortable balance between reading speed, comprehension, remembering the gist and enjoyment. Small improvements count. Choose a pace that works for you and complete 30 practice sessions at your own rhythm—even if they take more than 30 days.
